How We Started

From a Friday newsletter to a daily destination.

TrendMint launched in 2021 as a Friday afternoon roundup, written by three former newsroom editors who were tired of watching good stories get buried under clickbait. It went out to forty people. Then four hundred. Then it stopped fitting in an email.

Today TrendMint covers celebrity news, for readers across more than sixty countries — but the editorial process hasn't changed. Every story still gets the same question asked of it: is this actually worth someone's time?

Our Mission

We exist to make trending news feel worth your time again — curated by editors, not algorithms, and written for readers, not pageviews.

02

Verify & Research

Every claim gets checked against primary sources before a single word of the article gets written.

FAQ

Questions readers ask us most.

How often do you publish new stories?
We publish roughly 40 new stories a week across all six categories, with breaking news going up as it happens.
Where do your stories and tips come from?
A mix of original reporting, verified press materials, and reader tips — every source is checked by an editor before publication.
Can I submit a story tip or pitch?
Yes — use the contact form and select "Pitch a story" in your message. Our editors review every submission personally.
What's your corrections policy?
If a story needs a correction, we update it and note the change clearly at the bottom of the article — no quiet edits.
Do you offer advertising or partnerships?
We do, on a limited basis that keeps sponsored content clearly labeled and separate from editorial coverage. Reach out via the contact page.
Is there a newsletter?
Yes — the same Friday roundup that started it all is still going out weekly. Sign-up details are on the contact page.
